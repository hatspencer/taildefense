# Recorded sounds

Every file here is CC0, cut from the packs below: a sound with recordings plays them instead of
its recipe in `../bank.ts`. Each cut starts at the given second of its source, runs no longer
than the recipe's sound did, fades out over its last 30%, and is levelled to the loudness of the
recipe it replaces (its loudest 50 ms). Guns are soft-saturated first, as the recipes are, and
some creatures are slowed to deepen them (the abomination, brute and broodmother).
Guns are 44.1 kHz 96 kbps mono MP3, the rest 32 kHz 56 kbps.

Recipes kept: explosions, cannon, rockets, tesla, crowbar, sirens, radio, reloads, the UI.
