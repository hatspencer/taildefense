the page linked, its licence read on that page, then cut, levelled and encoded by the steps
listed: decoded to mono, optionally pitched or filtered, cut at an onset, faded out (or
crossfaded into a seamless loop), levelled so its loudest 50 ms match the recipe in
`../bank.ts` it plays with (soft-clipped, never more than ~6 dB into the clipper), and
encoded to MP3. Player guns are not here.
